691 - the floor at corpus scale, and a frame that verified itself
=================================================================

out/689 placed a floor at 250 px to separate an equation's internal
gaps from the gap before a right-set number, on a valley SIX ROWS
WIDE. pdfdrill's corpus pass then made the list 107x larger — 272,442
display rows over 1,667 documents — so the valley could be checked
against a real population.

It held. And getting there cost a retraction.

====================================================================
1. WHAT COULD BE MEASURED AT ALL
====================================================================

    display rows in the list              272,442
    rows whose page is RENDERED            26,883  (9.9%)
    sampled, seed 690                       4,000

An ink measurement needs a rendered page. 983 of 1,667 documents have a
render directory and 6,349 of 14,500 needed pages exist, so this is a
tenth of the list — and NOT a random tenth: renders exist for the
documents somebody chose to work on, which are the maths-dense ones.
Every figure below is of that subset.

====================================================================
2. THE FLOOR HELD
====================================================================

    widest interior gap, 4,000 rows
      p50 26   p90 89   p95 156   p97 323   p99 689   max 1,919

      0-50   2,963 | 50-100  487 | 100-150 153 | 150-190  31
    190-250     41 | 250-300   15 | 300-400  25 | 400-600  51
    600-900     37 | 900-2000  11

250-300 is 15 rows between 41 and 25 — a local minimum on 3,990 rows
where sigma26 gave 6. The floor sits above the LABELLED CEILING of 188
(the widest interior gap over 202 rows whose number was already split
out, so known to be body only) and inside that minimum.

    >= 190 px   180 rows (4.5%)
    >= 250 px   141 (3.5%)
    >= 300 px   128 (3.2%)

Shipped as `NUMBER_FLOOR_PX = 250`, with the two populations asserted
rather than the number: `tests/test_eqink.py` fails if the constant
moves below the labelled ceiling or outside the measured minimum.

====================================================================
3. THE RETRACTION — A FRAME THAT COULD NOT FAIL ITS OWN TEST
====================================================================

The first pass reported 181 of 3,995 rows (4.5%) with NO INK in their
declared region, against 0 of 2,549 on sigma26. It was the mapping.

I put the list's regions through `page_frames`, which exists for
MATHPIX page pixels. The list's regions come from the PDF page. On a
document whose page size differs from the one MathPix rastered, that
frame carries an inset — 504 x 492 px on cardona p14 — and every region
lands in the bottom margin.

    cardona p14, `a\in\mathrm{Sp}(a)`
      through page_frames     0 ink px, the bottom margin
      pure 400/250, no inset  890 ink px, and the crop reads ∫_{a∈Sp(a)}

    the 181 "no ink" rows, re-measured   171 recover, 10 remain (0.25%)

THE PART WORTH KEEPING IS WHY IT SURVIVED VERIFICATION. I had checked
this mapping by eye on four sigma26 rows and all four landed perfectly.
sigma26's frames are (1.6, 1.6, 0, 0) — the broken mapping and the
correct one are IDENTICAL there. The check was run on the population
where the bug cannot appear.

A HYPOTHESIS RETRACTED TOO. I attributed the inset to a CropBox/MediaBox
difference, quoting a row as reporting `page_width_pt: 411.4` beside
MediaBox-origin coordinates. It does not reproduce: that row reads
595.0 x 842.0, one row, file unmodified. The real cause is MIXED PAGE
SIZES — cardona p1 is 411x620 pt and its body is A4 — and the rows
report each page correctly. Said before defending it.

pdfdrill then found the same shape on their side, larger: their list
carried ONE page size per document from `pdfinfo`, so "region past its
page" read 16,729 rows (6.14%) and is 199 (0.07%) against the per-page
map. My own 1,501 width-only count used their per-row page width and
inherited the same denominator.

====================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
====================================================================

  * 9.9% of the list, and a biased 9.9%. A document nobody rendered is
    a document this cannot see.
  * The 188 px ceiling is still from 202 rows of ONE publisher. It is
    the only LABELLED population available — rows whose number another
    tool had already removed — and it has not grown.
  * The floor separates a gap from a number. It does not say the
    right-hand group IS a number: out/689's `∅ ⟵——— [1,n]` is a
    diagram whose right group is 115 px, the width of a real `(4.1)`.
    Ink finds the separation; only the content test confirms it.
  * 10 rows still have no ink in their region. Not yet diagnosed, and
    reported as 10 rows rather than as a class.
